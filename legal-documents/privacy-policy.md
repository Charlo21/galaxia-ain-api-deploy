We use collected information to:

- Comply with legal and regulatory obligations (KYC/AML, tax reporting)
- Detect and prevent fraud, money laundering, and other illegal activities
- Communicate with you about the Service
- Respond to complaints and support requests
- Ensure network security and integrity

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR)

For EU users, we process your data based on:
- **Contract**: To provide the Service you requested
- **Legal Obligation**: To comply with AML, KYC, and tax regulations
- **Legitimate Interest**: For fraud prevention and security
- **Consent**: Where you have provided explicit consent

## 6. Data Retention

### 6.1 Retention Periods
- **Transaction Records**: Minimum 5 years (AML requirement)
- **KYC Documents**: 5 years after account closure

- **Support Communications**: 3 years

### 6.2 Deletion
We will delete your data after retention periods expire, except where:
- Legal hold applies
- Ongoing investigation
- Required by law

## 7. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are in the EU, you have the right to:
- **Access**: Request a copy of your personal data
- **Rectification**: Correct inaccurate data
- **Erasure**: Request deletion (subject to legal requirements)
- **Portability**: Receive your data in a portable format
- **Object**: Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Restrict**: Request restriction of processing
- **Withdraw Consent**: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise these rights, contact: privacy@galaxia.io
